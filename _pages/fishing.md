# Fishing

I’m an avid fisherman who spends much of my free time fly fishing across Montana, Idaho, and Wyoming. 

From early spring runoff on the Gallatin to late-season browns on the Madison, fishing is where I reset. I’m especially drawn to rivers that demand technical casting, careful reading of water, and patience.
